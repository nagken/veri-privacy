Privacy Policy — Veri — Is This Real?

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Developer: Neospark LLC (“Veri by Neospark”)
Contact: veribyneospark@gmail.com

Veri — Is This Real? (“Veri”, “the app”) helps you check suspicious texts, emails, job offers, shopping deals, news posts, direct messages, contracts, and AI-generated media for signs of a scam. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Veri.

The short version: Veri works on your device. What you check stays on your device.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. Veri has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your content.

  • Content you check (pasted text, screenshots, and media you choose to analyze) is processed entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Check history is stored only in the app’s local storage on your device, so you can review your past checks. You can delete your history at any time inside the app, and deleting the app removes it entirely.
  • No analytics or tracking. This version of Veri contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.
  • No purchases. This version of Veri is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Information we share

We share nothing, because we receive nothing. We do not sell personal information. We have no personal information to sell.

Permissions

Veri may ask for access to your photos or camera only so you can pick or capture an image for a check. Images you select are analyzed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

Veri is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children — or from anyone.

Security

Because your content never leaves your device, it is protected by your device’s own security (passcode, biometrics, and operating-system storage protections). No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but Veri’s design removes the largest risk: there is no Veri database of your checks to breach.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Veri ever changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account or cloud feature), we will update this policy and the store data-safety declarations before that version is released, and the app will describe the change honestly.
